# PreTeXt Codespace

Welcome to my math for low vision/blind students (LVBS) repo. This is the space where I am developing a PreTeXtbook (and will extend it to a full course at some point) for the liberal arts math course special section for low-vision/blind students that I teach at FSU. This book covers topics that satisfiy the State of Florida requirements for the course. Reach out if you have questions. 

The next steps in development are to try to integrate this into the "regular" course. There are a few viable ideas for this - will update as they are approved. The principles behind this development are UDL and LCT. Universal Design for Learning ensures that multiple modalities are available to students, and Learner-Centered Teaching implies that teaching is about the learner, and not the teacher, thus requiring feedback from students on their learning. PreTeXt, I believe, is the right tool for the job along with the other PROSE partners, of course. To note, PROSE is also OER (Open Education Resources).

Since the LVBS will not take only math courses, this effort extends to other courses as well with general chemistry 1 comping up soon. You may see a second repo for that soon. 

If you are working on similar things, please feel free to reach out. I would love to collaborate in this development to make learning posiible and enjoyable to LVBS as our other students. Many thanks to the PreTeXt, (PROSE in general), prefigure, and other teams for developing these tools and regularly supporting the community through multiple modalities.
